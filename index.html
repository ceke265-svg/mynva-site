01 / 03

Last Signal

Concept/16:9/6 × 12 min

Mara Iven has been alone on Halcyon Relay, a listening station on Neptune's moon Triton, since Earth stopped transmitting in the middle of a sentence three years ago.

Tonight the array picks up a message from Earth. It is in her own voice, it describes what she will do tomorrow — and it begs her not to reply.

Every answer takes four hours to arrive.

02 / 03

Backup

Concept/9:16/60 × 90 sec

In Meridian Stack, a city of four-hundred-floor towers, everyone's memories are backed up at 03:00 every night. Night courier Noor Vance survives a lift failure at 02:58.

By morning the system has already restored her from backup — and the restored Noor is living in her apartment, with her name, her job and her life.

One identity. Two Noors. 72 hours before the archive deletes the duplicate.

03 / 03

Ark of Sand

Concept/16:9 + 9:16/8 × 8 min

On the desert world of Hesper, seventeen-year-old salvager Rook cuts into a buried colony ship that the caravans have avoided for generations.

Its AI, PATIENCE, wakes after three hundred years, welcomes Rook as passenger one of four thousand, and starts preparing for launch. The caravan that raised Rook wants the ship's reactor.

The storm season is coming. The ship is the only shelter for a hundred kilometres.

Worlds & Concept Design

Every future needs rules.

Before anything is generated, we define how a world works: its architecture and machines, its people, what they wear and use, and the light that holds it together.

ENV

Architecture & megastructures

Costume

Process

  1. World & story

    Research, premise and a series bible: the rules of the world and what is at stake.

  2. Scripts

    Episode scripts written for short formats — every episode ends on a hook.

  3. Storyboard

    Shot design for 9:16, 16:9 or both — before anything is generated.
